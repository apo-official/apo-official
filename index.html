APO SAFETY CENTER

Know what to do.
Stay safe.

APO is an independent community, not law enforcement. Use these sections for community rules, reporting guidance, account safety, and help when something feels wrong.

01

No doxxing

Never post addresses, phone numbers, private documents, or other private identifying information.

02

No threats or harassment

Report harmful behavior. Do not threaten, intimidate, dogpile, or retaliate against anyone.

03

Never post illegal content

Do not upload, save, forward, or redistribute sexual content involving minors. Report it through the appropriate platform or authority instead.

04

Evidence matters

Do not make public accusations without reliable evidence. Use private reporting channels rather than public callouts.

05

Respect members

No hate, bullying, targeted harassment, spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

06

Protect survivors

Be respectful. Never pressure someone to publicly share personal experiences or private information.

07

Follow moderation

Community moderators enforce APO's rules and may remove content that puts members at risk.

08

Report, don't retaliate

Use official reporting channels. APO members should not confront or conduct vigilante investigations.

Report a community safety concern

Don't include illegal sexual images or other prohibited material. A link or plain-text description is enough.

1

Stop the conversation

If an interaction makes you uncomfortable or unsafe, you do not have to keep responding.

2

Block the account

Use the platform's blocking tools so the account cannot keep contacting you.

3

Use official reporting

Report the account or content directly to the platform. For serious or immediate danger, involve a trusted adult and appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement services.

4

Don't confront or expose them

Don't threaten, dox, publicly shame, or arrange an in-person meeting. Keep yourself out of danger.

PRIVACY

Keep personal details private

Avoid posting your address, school, phone number, passwords, or other sensitive information.

SECURITY

Protect your login

Use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication when the service supports it.

CONTACT

Watch for impersonation

APO staff should not ask you for passwords, payment, or private account credentials.

LINKS

Be careful with unknown links

Don't sign in through suspicious links or download unexpected files sent by strangers.
