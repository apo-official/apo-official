APO SAFETY CENTER

Know what to do.
Stay safe.

APO is an independent community, not law enforcement. These tools cover community rules, responsible reporting, immediate steps, and account safety.

01

No doxxing

Never post addresses, phone numbers, private documents, or other identifying information.

02

No threats or harassment

Report harmful behavior instead of threatening, intimidating or retaliating.

03

Never post illegal content

Do not upload, save, forward, or redistribute sexual content involving minors.

04

Evidence matters

Use private reporting channels and avoid unsupported public accusations.

05

Respect members

No hate, targeted harassment, bullying, spam, or deliberate disruption.

06

Protect survivors

Never pressure someone to publicly share private experiences or personal details.

07

Follow moderation

Moderators may remove content or users that break APO community rules.

08

Report, don’t retaliate

Use official reporting tools. Do not confront, threaten, expose, or arrange meetings.

Report a community safety concern

Do not upload or redistribute illegal sexual content. A link or plain-text description is enough.

1

Stop the conversation

You do not need to keep responding to someone who makes you uncomfortable or unsafe.

2

Block the account

Use the platform’s blocking tools to stop further contact.

3

Use official reporting

Report the account or content to the platform. For immediate danger, involve a trusted adult and appropriate local emergency services.

4

Don’t confront or expose them

Do not threaten, dox, publicly shame, or arrange an in-person meeting.

PRIVACY

Keep personal details private

Avoid posting your address, school, phone number, passwords, or sensitive information.

SECURITY

Protect your login

Use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication where available.

CONTACT

Watch for impersonation

APO staff should never ask for passwords, payment, or private account credentials.

LINKS

Be careful with unknown links

Do not sign in through suspicious links or download unexpected files from strangers.

ABOUT APO

A focused community.
A clearer mission.

APO was founded by Guru Prime in 2025. It is an independent online community centered on safer spaces, responsible reporting, and communication.

MISSION

Safer spaces

APO’s community rules prioritize privacy, evidence, respect, survivor protection, and responsible moderation.

BOUNDARY

Not law enforcement

APO is not a police or investigative agency. Serious concerns should be directed to platforms and appropriate authorities.

CONTACT SAFETY

Know who you’re talking to.

APO staff should never ask you for passwords, payment, or private credentials. Be cautious with unexpected messages or requests to move to another app.
