What we do

Three things, done properly.

Expose and report

When we find a predator, we document it and report it to the platform and to the authorities. Reports go to the people who can actually remove accounts and start investigations.

Show our work in videos

Our videos show how predators operate and how the reporting process works, so parents, teens and other communities can recognise the same patterns.

Keep people safe

We share safety guides, answer questions, and support anyone who has been targeted. Nobody who comes to us for help is ever blamed.

How to report a predator

Reports made through the right channels are the ones that lead to action. Follow these steps.

  1. Report it to the platform.

    Use the report tool on the app or site. For Discord, use the form at discord.com/safety and choose Child Safety. Include user IDs, the server ID and message links.

  2. Report it to the CyberTipline.

    Send a report to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org. In the UK, use the Internet Watch Foundation.

  3. Tell the police.

    Send a tip to the FBI at tips.fbi.gov, or contact your local police. Outside the US, use your country's police or child protection agency.

Do

  • Save user IDs, usernames and message links
  • Write down dates and times
  • Block the account after you report it
  • Tell a trusted adult if a child is involved

Don't

  • Download, save or share sexual content involving minors. Possessing it is illegal, even as evidence
  • Confront or threaten the person
  • Post someone's personal information (doxxing)
  • Accuse someone without evidence

If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US).

Stay safe online

Predators rely on secrecy and pressure. Knowing the signs takes away their advantage.

For kids and teens

  • Keep personal info private. Never share your address, school, phone number or real photos with someone you only know online.
  • Watch for secrets. If someone asks you to keep your chat a secret, that's a red flag.
  • Be careful with private chats. Be wary if someone pushes you to move to a private app or asks for pictures.
  • Trust your gut. If a chat feels wrong, stop, block, and tell someone you trust.
  • It is never your fault. If you're being pressured or blackmailed, you can still ask for help.

For parents

  • Talk early and often. Make it easy for your child to tell you about anything online without fear of punishment.
  • Know the apps. Learn which apps and servers they use, and check privacy and messaging settings together.
  • Learn the warning signs. Sudden secrecy, gifts from unknown people, or a new "older friend" online.
  • Save evidence, then report. Keep usernames and message links, and report to the platform and the CyberTipline.

Need help right now?

  • NCMEC hotline: 1-800-843-5678
  • Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline: 1-800-422-4453
  • Images of you shared without consent: takeitdown.ncmec.org

Community rules

These keep APO safe, and keep our work credible.

  1. No doxxing.Never share addresses, phone numbers or private details of anyone.
  2. No threats or harassment.We report people. We don't hurt or threaten them.
  3. Never post illegal content.No sexual content involving minors, ever. Report it instead.
  4. Evidence first.Don't accuse anyone without proof. A false accusation harms real people.
  5. Respect each other.No hate, no bullying, no drama.
  6. Protect survivors.Be kind and never pressure anyone to share their story.
  7. Follow staff.Moderators keep the community safe. Their decisions stand.
  8. Report, don't retaliate.Use the official channels and leave the rest to the authorities.

These are the community's general rules. The full, current rules are in the APO Discord server.

Questions

Is APO a law enforcement agency?

No. APO is an independent community. We pass what we find to the platforms and authorities who can act on it.

Do you dox people?

No. Doxxing is against our rules. We document and report, and we never publish personal information like home addresses or phone numbers.

I've found a predator. What should I do?

Follow the steps in the reporting section above: report to the platform, then the CyberTipline, then the police. Save user IDs and message links, and don't save or share any sexual content involving minors.

Is APO connected to Discord or any other platform?

No. APO is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any platform.

How do I join the community?

Use the Discord button on this page. Read the rules, introduce yourself, and ask staff if you need anything.
