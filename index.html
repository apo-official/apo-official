Safety first.

APO should never require someone to put themselves at risk to gather proof.

Important: Do not download, save, request, or redistribute sexual content involving minors. Do not arrange in-person confrontations. Preserve only lawful, necessary information such as usernames, URLs, timestamps, and report IDs, then use official reporting channels.
